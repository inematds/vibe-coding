What Vibe Coding in Practice is

🗣️

You describe it, the AI builds it

Instead of learning the mechanics of each node or line of code, you describe the desired outcome. AI selects tools, configures them, and debugs.

🔁

Iterate through conversation

When something needs to change, you simply ask. The Plan → Build → Troubleshoot → Optimize cycle becomes your building routine.

🚀

From zero to product

Go from your first local workflow to a real app online—with automatic deploys, authentication, security, and recurring payments.

🎯 Who it's for

People who want to build automations and applications with AI by chatting — even without knowing how to program. Prerequisites: a computer, free accounts with the tools used, and a willingness to iterate. No prior programming knowledge required.
